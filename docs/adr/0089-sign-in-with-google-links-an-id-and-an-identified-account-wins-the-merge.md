# ADR-0089 — Sign in with Google links an id, and an identified account wins the merge

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-09-28 (issue #280)
**Depends on:** [ADR-0003](./0003-anonymous-first-identity-with-email-recovery.md), [ADR-0009](./0009-account-merge-recomputes-from-the-union-of-completions.md), [ADR-0022](./0022-opaque-session-tokens-in-a-sessions-table.md), [ADR-0048](./0048-the-streak-is-a-client-fetched-server-computed-value.md), [ADR-0049](./0049-account-merge-one-pure-function-one-idempotent-operation.md), [ADR-0050](./0050-email-attach-magic-link-tokens-consents-and-the-lgpd-minimum.md)
**Amends:** the founding handoff (*"lançar **sem** login social"*, and *"Login social … implementação pós-lançamento"*) and ADR-0003 (*"Social login … remains a post-launch upgrade"*): Google ships now, at Fernando's request of 2026-09-28; Apple stays post-launch. ADR-0049 decision 3 (the winner rule) and decision 4 (*"no code path mints a session against an existing user"*). ADR-0050 decision 1 (*"No flow variants"*), decision 5 (*"the holder is always older and therefore the winner"*), decision 14 and the Rejected item *"Merging the clicking browser's account implicitly"*.

## Context

`users.google_id` (nullable, unique) has existed since M0 for this. Signing in must carry a streak to any device, like the magic link, and be undone in Ajustes. A second identity handle that is also a credential changes what a merge may do: whatever handle survives on the merged account can open it.

## Decision

1. **An OAuth code flow run by `apps/api`, scope `openid` only.** `GET /auth/google/start` sets a `__Host-miolos_google` cookie (state + PKCE verifier, 10 minutes) and redirects to Google with `prompt=select_account`; `GET /auth/google/callback` Zod-parses `code` and `state`, checks the state, exchanges the code with the client secret, and keeps only `sub`. The ID token comes straight from Google's token endpoint over TLS, which stands in for its signature (OIDC Core 3.1.3.7). No nonce: PKCE, the state cookie and the server-side exchange already bind the flow. The `__Host-` prefix stops a sibling subdomain planting a flow.
2. **Resolution.** Holder = the account whose `google_id` is `sub`; requester = the presented session. No requester: the holder, or a new account linked to `sub`. No holder: link `sub` to the requester (one Google per account, else `conflict`). A requester holding an identity handle **switches** to the holder and merges nothing (`switched`). An anonymous requester merges into the holder. This is a second recovery flow beside the magic link, and it merges the clicking browser's account: acceptable here because the flow cookie binds start and callback to the one browser whose player pressed the button and picked the Google account, so no forwarded link can reach a bystander. For the same reason ADR-0050 decision 14's acknowledgement gate is not repeated; the card says before the click that this device's anonymous games move to the Google account.
3. **Sessions.** Every success mints a fresh session, then deletes the presented one. An anonymous requester's other sessions are deleted after the link or merge: Google never proved them, and a planted one would otherwise open the Google account (the same argument as ADR-0050 decision 13, narrowed to the unproven side). The holder's sessions and an identified requester's are kept, so a sign-in does not sign every other device out. The session is minted against the account holding `google_id = sub`: a handle holder, which `mergeAccounts` never makes a loser (decision 4), so it is never a tombstone.
4. **The winner rule (amends ADR-0049 decision 3).** An account holding an identity handle beats one holding none; then older `created_at`; then lower id. **Two accounts that both hold a handle are never merged**: `mergeAccounts` throws `isIdentityClashError` before any write, and `POST /attach/confirm` answers 409 `sign-in-attached`. Merging them would leave one side's sign-in on the other side's data. So a loser never holds a handle, and ADR-0049 decision 4's tombstone statement stays as a race backstop only.
5. **Its own read.** `GET /account/google` returns `unavailable | unlinked | linked`, a new strict contract per ADR-0048 decision 3. It reads `linked` even when unconfigured, so a linked player can always unlink. `POST /account/unlink-google` nulls the id; Ajustes re-reads the state before offering the sign-in again.
6. **Dormant until keyed.** Without `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `WEB_ORIGIN`, start and callback answer 503 before reading anything, and Ajustes shows no card.
7. **No telemetry event.** ADR-0069's `login_linked` stays the magic link's; a Google event waits for a question it would answer.

## Consequences

- ADR-0050 decision 5's crash residual now also covers an older anonymous requester's whole history in the email flow, since the holder wins at any age. Same repair seam, same revisit trigger. A crash in the Google merge arm has the same residual; a retried sign-in resolves as holder = requester and does not re-run the merge.
- A player with Google on one account and an email on another cannot join them; they unlink one first.
- An identified requester can still link a Google it proved while a planted session is in the browser, as the email flow can attach; Ajustes shows whose account it is before the click.
- A Google-linked account without an email still gets the streak-5 email prompt; email is also the reminder channel.
- The only data from Google is `sub`. `/privacidade` says what it is and what it is for.
