# ADR-0089 — Sign in with Google links an id, and an identified account wins the merge

**Status:** Accepted — 2026-09-28 (issue #280)
**Depends on:** [ADR-0003](./0003-anonymous-first-identity-with-email-recovery.md), [ADR-0009](./0009-account-merge-recomputes-from-the-union-of-completions.md), [ADR-0022](./0022-opaque-session-tokens-in-a-sessions-table.md), [ADR-0048](./0048-the-streak-is-a-client-fetched-server-computed-value.md), [ADR-0049](./0049-account-merge-one-pure-function-one-idempotent-operation.md), [ADR-0050](./0050-email-attach-magic-link-tokens-consents-and-the-lgpd-minimum.md)
**Amends:** the founding handoff (*"lançar **sem** login social"*, and *"Login social … implementação pós-lançamento"*) and ADR-0003 (*"Social login … remains a post-launch upgrade"*): Google ships now, at Fernando's request of 2026-09-28; Apple stays post-launch. ADR-0009's tombstoning consequence as narrowed by ADR-0050 (*"outside a confirm no session is ever deleted"*). ADR-0049 decision 3 (the winner rule) and decision 4 (*"delete is forbidden"* and *"no code path mints a session against an existing user"*). ADR-0050's header premise (*"sessions are never deleted in v1 outside decision 13"*), decision 1 (*"No flow variants"*), decision 4 (*"The clicking browser is authenticated AS the winner, never merged into it"*), decision 5 (*"the holder is always older and therefore the winner"*), decision 14 and the Rejected item *"Merging the clicking browser's account implicitly"*.

## Context

`users.google_id` (nullable, unique) has existed since M0 for this. Signing in must carry a streak to any device, like the magic link, and be undone in Ajustes. A second identity handle that is also a credential changes what a merge may do: whatever handle survives on the merged account can open it.

## Decision

1. **An OAuth code flow run by `apps/api`, scope `openid` only.** `GET /auth/google/start` sets a `__Host-miolos_google` cookie (state + PKCE verifier, 10 minutes) and redirects to Google with `prompt=select_account`; `GET /auth/google/callback` Zod-parses `code` and `state`, checks the state, exchanges the code with the client secret, and keeps only `sub`. The ID token comes straight from Google's token endpoint over TLS, which stands in for its signature (OIDC Core 3.1.3.7). No nonce: PKCE, the state cookie and the server-side exchange already bind the flow. The `__Host-` prefix stops a sibling subdomain planting a flow.
2. **Resolution.** Holder = the account whose `google_id` is `sub`; requester = the presented session. No requester, or the requester is the holder: sign in to the holder, or to a new account linked to `sub`. A requester holding an identity handle **switches** to the holder and merges nothing (`switched`); with no holder, `sub` is linked to it in place (one Google per account, else `conflict`). An **anonymous requester is never linked in place**: it merges into the holder, or into a new account linked to `sub`. This is a second recovery flow beside the magic link, and it merges the clicking browser's account: acceptable here because the flow cookie binds start and callback to the one browser whose player pressed the button and picked the Google account, so no forwarded link can reach a bystander. For the same reason ADR-0050 decision 14's acknowledgement gate is not repeated; the card says before the click that this device's anonymous games move to the Google account, and that its other devices sign in with Google too.
3. **Sessions.** The callback mints one fresh session, always on the account holding `google_id = sub`, and sets the cookie only if that session still exists. Signing in: the fresh session is minted on the holder, the callback checks it still holds `sub` (else the fresh session is deleted), and the presented session is deleted. The holder's other sessions and an identified requester's are kept, so a sign-in does not sign every other device out. Anonymous requester: the fresh session is minted on the Google account, **every** session of the anonymous account is deleted, and only then does it merge, holding no session. Nothing the anonymous account holds was proven by this Google account, so a planted session never reaches it (ADR-0050 decision 13's argument, applied before the merge rather than after). Its pending magic links then point at a tombstone and answer 410. A handle holder is never a merge loser (decision 4), so no session lands on a tombstone.
4. **The winner rule (amends ADR-0049 decision 3).** An account holding an identity handle beats one holding none; then older `created_at`; then lower id. **Two accounts that both hold a handle are never merged**: `mergeAccounts` throws `isIdentityClashError` before any write, and `POST /attach/confirm` answers 409 `sign-in-attached`. Merging them would leave one side's sign-in on the other side's data. So a loser never holds a handle; ADR-0049 decision 4's tombstone statement stays as the backstop for a handle that lands on the loser mid-merge, and a test drives that race.
5. **Its own read.** `GET /account/google` returns `unavailable | unlinked | linked`, a new strict contract per ADR-0048 decision 3. It reads `linked` even when unconfigured, so a linked player can always unlink. `POST /account/unlink-google` nulls the id; Ajustes re-reads the state before offering the sign-in again.
6. **Dormant until keyed.** Without `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `WEB_ORIGIN`, start and callback answer 503 before reading anything, and Ajustes shows no card.
7. **No telemetry event, no consent row.** ADR-0069's `login_linked` stays the magic link's. Linking Google is the player's own act to open the account, not ADR-0050's recovery consent: it writes no `consent_events` row, and /privacidade says what the id is for.

## Consequences

- ADR-0050 decision 5's crash residual now also covers an older anonymous requester's whole history in the email flow, since the holder wins at any age. Same repair seam, same revisit trigger. A crash in the Google merge arm leaves the anonymous account without sessions and unmerged; its games stay in the database for that repair seam.
- The anonymous account's other devices lose their session and re-mint an empty identity. They get the games back by signing in with Google there too; the card says so.
- A player with Google on one account and an email on another cannot join them; `/vincular` says so and points to Ajustes to unlink Google first.
- An anonymous device's push subscription still follows it into the Google account. A planted one would receive that account's streak-at-risk reminders, and nothing else; deleting it would silently mute the real device.
- Linking Google onto an identified account is the intended "add Google" act, and Ajustes shows the account's email above the card before the click. A planted identified session is the email flow's residual too, since `/vincular` refuses a different email.
- A holder that unlinks Google while a merge into it runs keeps the merged games; the sign-in answers `failed`, and the device keeps its fresh session there, where its games now are.
- A Google-linked account without an email still gets the streak-5 email prompt; email is also the reminder channel.
- The only data from Google is `sub`. `/privacidade` says what it is and what it is for.
